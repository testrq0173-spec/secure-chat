Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Blinded capability queues. Zero digital footprints.

Unlike WhatsApp or Signal where the server knows who sent a message to whom, Bharosa uses unidirectional blinded queue tokens. The server acts as a blind postbox.

Step 01

Queue Capability Generation

Each contact pair creates two decoupled unidirectional random 128-bit queue capabilities (A→B and B→A). Neither queue reveals the other.

Step 02

Double Outer Encryption

Packets are sealed first with the contact's Double Ratchet key, then double-wrapped with the transport-level queue capability key.

Step 03

Zero-Retention Wipe

As soon as the recipient device pulls the packet, it is instantly deleted from the relay server with zero logs or footprints.

Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Bharosa's privacy and setup.

Do I need a phone number or email address to use Bharosa? +
No. Bharosa never asks for your phone number, email, or any identifying credentials. Your identity is a cryptographic keypair created directly on your device.
How do I connect with friends and contacts? +
You connect via QR Code scan or by sharing a single-use connection invitation link. Once connected, your devices establish blinded queues without central registration.
When will the iOS version be released? +
The iOS version is currently undergoing final internal testing on Apple TestFlight and will be launched on the App Store shortly. Android users can download the ready-to-run APK right now.
Can the server operator or ISP read my chats or media? +
No. All messages, photos, videos, voice notes, and files are protected with Double Ratchet end-to-end encryption. The server only sees encrypted ciphertext and drops the packet as soon as it is delivered.
